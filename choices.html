Your GameKey privacy choices

You can use GameKey's core cheat and map reference features without creating a GameKey account. Some information is stored locally on your device, and optional advertising-related settings may apply when live ads are enabled.

Local favorites and preferences

GameKey stores favorite cheats, language preferences, downloaded reference data, and certain app settings on your device. You can generally remove this local data by deleting the app. Deleting and reinstalling GameKey may reset your preferences.

Advertising consent

Current status: GameKey's pre-release mock advertisements do not serve live Google advertising. Live AdMob serving is currently disabled.

If Google AdMob is enabled in a future production version, applicable consent and privacy options may be provided through GameKey → Settings → Advertising & Privacy. The options shown depend on location, applicable law, and the messages configured in AdMob.

If you see an iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, you may allow or deny the requested permission. On iPhone, available app tracking controls can also be reviewed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Denying tracking does not necessarily disable all advertising.

Privacy requests

To learn more about technical information involved in game-content updates or future advertising, read the Privacy Policy. We will publish a private, monitored contact method for privacy inquiries before GameKey's public App Store launch. Do not submit personal information to public GitHub issues.

Information processed independently by Google, Supabase, Apple, or GitHub may also be subject to the respective providers' privacy settings and rights processes.
