1. Who operates the app

Cheats for GTA 6 (the “App”) is an independent, unofficial Grand Theft Auto cheat-code and map reference guide operated by the publisher named above (“we,” “us,” or “our”). The App is not made, sponsored, endorsed by, or affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS App and any Android version we may release. It describes both current core functionality and Google AdMob banner and full-screen interstitial advertising when enabled. If we materially change our practices, we will update this policy and applicable store disclosures.

2. Information kept on your device

You do not need to create an account to use the App. The App stores selected language, favorite cheats, certain cheat catalogs and update timestamps, and advertising frequency/settings information locally on your device. These preferences are not intended to be uploaded to an account or synchronized across devices.

Most historical GTA cheat codes are built into the App. GTA V and GTA VI maps are bundled with the current app. Removing the App or clearing its storage normally removes local favorites, preferences and cached content; device backups may retain copies subject to your device-provider settings.

3. GTA VI updates and remote map images

The App checks a read-only Supabase service for published GTA VI cheat information and advertising configuration. These requests can expose standard technical connection information, such as an IP address, request time, app/network metadata, and error information, to Supabase and its infrastructure providers. The App does not require a Supabase login and does not intentionally send your favorites to Supabase.

Maps for older GTA titles may load from externally hosted map image URLs (currently Grand Theft Wiki image servers). When you open those maps, the hosting service may receive your IP address and ordinary web request metadata. GTA V and GTA VI maps are bundled locally in the current version. Opening a policy link or other external website also creates a connection to that site's provider.

Requests and any server logs are governed by the relevant service provider's retention and privacy practices. We do not control all external-server retention periods.

We do not promise that rejecting personalized advertising removes all ads.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), Google's advertising information (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads), and Google's advertising controls (https://myadcenter.google.com/).
