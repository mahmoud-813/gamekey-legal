1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how GameKey ("GameKey," "we," "our," or "the App") handles information when you use our iOS gaming companion app and this legal website. GameKey is an independent, unofficial guide for Grand Theft Auto titles.

Current development status: GameKey is in pre-release testing. Its demonstration ad previews are simulated and do not serve paid advertising. We have prepared Google AdMob integration for a future launch, but production advertising is currently disabled through our remote settings. This policy describes both the current features and what we expect to happen if we activate actual advertising. We will update it if the implementation changes.

2. Data stored on your device

GameKey does not require you to register an account or provide a name, password, or email address to look up cheat codes. The App stores certain preferences locally using your device's app storage, such as:

  • Favorite cheat selections.
  • Your selected language and related preferences.
  • Downloaded GTA VI reference information and timestamps used to decide when to check for updates.
  • Advertising configuration cache and local counters used to limit ad frequency when real advertisements are enabled.

These settings are not currently uploaded to an individual GameKey user profile. You can generally remove locally stored information by deleting the App from your device. Reinstalling the App may reset your favorites and preferences.

3. Supabase content and configuration requests

The App connects to Supabase to check for updates to GTA VI reference content and to retrieve remotely configured advertising rules. These requests can involve technical network information such as your IP address, request time, device or network metadata, and service logs handled by Supabase or related hosting providers. We use these services to deliver updated content, maintain availability, troubleshoot issues, and set advertising limits.

The current implementation does not require a GameKey account and does not intentionally send your list of favorites, language choice, or cheat searches to a GameKey user profile. Supabase's processing and log retention are governed by its applicable policies and configurations. See Supabase Privacy Policy.

4. Current and future advertising

At present, production ads are disabled. The ad banners and full-screen screens displayed in Expo Go development previews are mockups only: they are not served by Google, do not represent live advertisers, and do not earn revenue.

We plan to offer GameKey free of charge and may enable Google AdMob advertising in future production releases. Intended formats include a small banner near the bottom of the app and occasional full-screen (interstitial) advertisements at suitable transitions. We plan to start with low frequency and may adjust how often ads appear over time. Ad frequency may be controlled using a remotely updated policy; there is no promise of a fixed number of ads.

When Google AdMob is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may automatically receive or process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from your connection.
  • Device information, operating-system version, app information, and identifiers permitted by your device settings and consent choices.
  • Ad requests, ad views, interactions, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Diagnostic, performance, and security information.

Depending on location, device settings, and permissions, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Apple App Tracking Transparency permission will be requested if required for tracking; declining a tracking request does not necessarily mean no ads will appear. We will not intentionally send your favorites or personal cheat-search history to advertisers to build a GameKey user profile.

Learn more through Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Consent and your choices

Where required, GameKey will present an advertising consent notice before requesting applicable advertisements. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may provide a privacy options form for reviewing or updating consent choices. When available, it can be opened from GameKey → Settings → Advertising & Privacy. The availability of this form may depend on your country and the messages configured for the App.

On iPhone, you can also review available tracking permissions in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Device-level advertising controls and applicable rights depend on your region and iOS version. You can stop use of the App and remove local preferences by uninstalling it.

If you wish to exercise a data protection right relating to information controlled by us, visit our Support page for the current contact options; a private contact channel will be added before public release. Requests concerning Google's or Supabase's independent processing may also need to be directed to those providers.

You can also read our Privacy Choices guide for a concise summary.

6. Why data may be processed

Technical information and local settings may be used to operate the App, remember your choices, download and cache game-reference content, prevent excessive advertising, deliver ads if enabled, measure ad functionality, prevent abuse, comply with law, and maintain the service.

We do not currently operate a GameKey user-account system or a standalone data-broker business. We do not sell your favorites or cheat searches as a customer list. Third-party advertising may involve data sharing as described above; we do not describe that sharing as anonymous or collection-free.

7. Retention, security, and deletion

On-device data remains until it is overwritten, cleared by the operating system, or the App is removed. Temporary catalog data is updated periodically. Technical logs held by service providers are retained according to their respective configurations and policies, which can differ from our on-device storage. AdMob and advertising partners may apply their own retention policies.

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the services we use, including restricting direct write access to the remote cheat catalog. However, no transmission or storage method can be guaranteed perfectly secure. If you need help with a privacy request, please use a private communication channel rather than posting personal details to a public GitHub issue.

8. Children

GameKey provides reference information about games that may contain mature themes. It is not designed for children or promoted as a children's app. We do not knowingly ask children to create accounts or submit personal information to GameKey. If a parent or guardian believes we have collected a child's personal information through a future feature, they may contact us once the support channel below is available.

9. International use and legal rights

The App and its service providers may process technical information in countries outside your own. Depending on where you live, applicable laws may provide rights to obtain information about, correct, delete, restrict, or object to particular processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. We will review appropriately submitted requests under applicable law and the nature of the data we actually control.

We do not claim that every data-rights mechanism is already available in this pre-release build. Relevant advertising consent and privacy-choice controls must be tested before launching paid ads in a region.

10. This GitHub Pages website

This legal website is a simple static site, without our own analytics scripts, advertising pixels, account registration, or form submissions. GitHub Pages and the underlying hosting infrastructure may process browser requests, IP addresses, or service logs as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Links to other websites are governed by their operators' policies.

11. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when app functionality, service providers, advertising practices, or laws change. The effective date at the top will be updated when changes are made. Material changes may also be communicated within the App where appropriate. The most current version is available at this URL.

12. Contact and privacy requests

For general questions, corrections, and support, visit GameKey Support. For non-sensitive questions you may use the public GitHub issue tracker linked there.

Our dedicated private contact address for personal privacy requests is being finalized ahead of GameKey’s public App Store release. Please do not publish personal or sensitive details in GitHub issues. We will update this page when the private contact method is available.
