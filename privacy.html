Privacy Policy

App version 1.0.0 · Last updated: November 13, 2026 · An offline copy is included in the app.

1. Who operates the app

Cheats for GTA 6: Guide (the “App”) is an independent, unofficial Grand Theft Auto cheat-code and map reference guide operated by Mahmoud Hasan (“we,” “us,” or “our”). The App is not made, sponsored, endorsed by, or affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

This Privacy Policy applies to the current iOS App and any Android version we may release. The current iOS version is free and does not contain or show advertisements. We may introduce advertising in a later app update; before activating it, we will review and update our disclosures to reflect the actual data practices.

2. Information kept on your device

You do not need to create an account to use the App. The App stores your selected language, favorite cheats, certain cached cheat catalogs, and content-update timestamps locally on your device. For occasional App Store review requests, the App also saves on this device the date of first use, number of app launches, number of completed browsing visits lasting at least 45 seconds, and dates of automatic review-request attempts (up to three in a rolling 365-day period). These counts and dates are not sent to us, Supabase, or advertising partners. Review App opens Apple's review service, and Share App uses your device's native sharing options; Apple and your chosen sharing service handle any information you choose to submit through them. There are no active advertising-frequency counters or Google AdMob ad requests in this ad-free release. Preferences are not intended to be uploaded to an account or synchronized across devices.

Most historical GTA cheat codes are built into the App. The GTA V map and an offline backup of the GTA VI map are bundled with the current app. A newer GTA VI map may be downloaded from Supabase Storage when available. Removing the App or clearing its storage normally removes local favorites, preferences and cached content; device backups may retain copies subject to your device-provider settings.

3. GTA VI updates and remote map images

The App checks a read-only Supabase service for published GTA VI cheat and map-version information. If a newer GTA VI map is available, the App may download that image from Supabase Storage. These requests can expose standard technical connection information, such as an IP address, request time, app/network metadata, and error information, to Supabase and its infrastructure providers. The App does not require a Supabase login and does not intentionally send your favorites to Supabase.

Maps for older GTA titles may load from externally hosted map image URLs (currently Grand Theft Wiki image servers). When you open those maps, the host may receive your IP address and ordinary web request metadata. The GTA V map is bundled locally. GTA VI uses its bundled map offline and can load a newer remotely hosted image. The Tips section includes original text bundled with the app and links to third-party guides or YouTube videos; those sites receive connection information only when you open them. Opening the public policy links likewise creates a connection to the website host.

Requests and any server logs are governed by the relevant service provider's retention and privacy practices. We do not control all external-server retention periods.

Cheats for GTA 6: Guide is currently free and does not display advertisements.
